Privacy Policy

Shorts Uploader · Last updated: 19 October 2026

Shorts Uploader is a personal tool used by the owner of the milkman69 YouTube channel to upload videos to that channel through the YouTube Data API.

Who this applies to

This tool is used only by the channel owner. It is not a public product and does not have other users.

What data is accessed

When the owner signs in with Google, Shorts Uploader receives permission to upload and manage videos on the owner's own YouTube channel. It stores the resulting OAuth access token on the owner's own system so uploads can run without signing in every time.

What we do not do

Shorts Uploader does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data from other people. It does not show ads, track visitors to this page, or send channel data to third parties beyond Google's own YouTube API.
